## ⚡ Why Rock-Quest?

Discord regularly releases promotional quests (Honkai: Star Rail, Marvel Rivals, Asphalt Legends, World of Warcraft, Roblox, etc.) that reward **Discord Orbs**, exclusive avatar decorations, or in-game codes when you play the game on desktop for 15 minutes.

Downloading full 80GB–120GB games just to get a 15-minute reward is a massive waste of time, bandwidth, and storage.

**Rock-Quest solves this completely:** It simulates the verified game process locally on your PC in standard user space. Discord detects the official game session, ticks your 15-minute quest progress to 100%, and lets you claim your Orbs instantly!

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### 1. Download & Install
Download the latest Windows installer:
* **[Download Rock-Quest Installer (v1.0.3)](https://quest.rfbd.shop/)**

### 2. Choose Your Quest
* Open **Rock-Quest**.
* Navigate to the **Quests** tab (or search for any verified game in the Library).
* Click **Start Quest**.

### 3. Let It Run for 15 Minutes
* The app will start the stand-in simulation.
* Keep Discord open — Discord will register you playing the verified game.
* Once the 15-minute timer finishes, open Discord > **User Settings** > **Gift Inventory** and claim your Orbs!
